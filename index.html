New release

Deck of Deception

How well do you really know your friends? When the chips are down and the economy tightens, loyalty is the first thing to vanish. Pixelaks Technologies invites you to step into Deck of Deception. This is not just a multiplayer card game. It is a psychological battlefield where trust is a liability and deception is your greatest weapon. You start the match with equal resources, but as the turns progress, the truth becomes twisted.

Action cards allow you to manipulate the table to your advantage. You can steal from your closest allies or completely drain their hard earned wealth into the bank. Will you play fair and build your fortune, or will you backstab your way to the top? The core loop demands tactical foresight. Hoard your cards and you will face heavy score penalties, but play too aggressively and you may find yourself totally bankrupt.

The ultimate test of friendship comes when a player loses all their coins. The game suddenly pauses, triggering an intense psychological showdown. The remaining players secretly agree on a hidden number and present a claim to the bankrupt player. They will look you in the virtual eye and lie to your face. Do you believe them? If you guess wrong, you are instantly eliminated from the table. Built with cutting edge web technology and featuring built in proximity voice chat, you can hear every lie as it happens. Step into the Syndicate, wager your Pixel Coins, and remember one simple rule. Trust no card.
