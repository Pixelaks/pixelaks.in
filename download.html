Adhyora For Android & PC

Welcome to the official Adhyora platform. The unified system for Students, Teachers, and Principals to manage attendance, internal marks, and college updates on the go or at your desk.

Installation Guide:

  1. Click the buttons below to visit the official Google Play Store or Microsoft Store.
  2. Select "Install" or "Get" to securely download the app to your device.
  3. Open Adhyora and select your Institution from the list.
  4. Log in using your registered credentials to access your dashboard.
