Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Business Name: Pixelaks Technologies (Sole Proprietorship)

Contact Email: pixelaks.technologies@gmail.com

All in-app purchases made within Pixelaks Games are for digital virtual goods (such as game items, coins, power-ups, unlockables, or premium features). These are consumed or applied to your game account immediately upon successful payment.

All sales of in-app purchases are final and non-refundable. Once a purchase is completed and the digital item or benefit has been delivered to your game account, we are unable to offer cancellations, refunds, or exchanges. This is consistent with standard industry practice for digital gaming content.

However, we will review refund requests in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • A technical error on our platform caused a payment to be charged without the in-app item being delivered.
  • A duplicate or double charge occurred for the same transaction due to a payment processing error.
  • An unauthorized transaction is reported promptly and supported by appropriate evidence.

To raise a refund concern under any of the above exceptions, please contact us at pixelaks.technologies@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction date with your Order ID, registered email, and a description of the issue. We will review and respond within 5–7 business days. Any approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method via Dodo Payments.

Pixelaks Technologies reserves the right to decline refund requests that do not fall within the exceptions listed above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
