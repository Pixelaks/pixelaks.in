Authentication & Onboarding

Welcome to Adhyora. Before accessing any portal, all users (Principals, Teachers, and Students) must pass through the central authentication gateway. The system uses specific credentials and codes to securely route users to their respective dashboards.

1. The Main Gateway

Upon launching Adhyora, you are greeted by the college selection screen.

  • Select Your College: Choose your institution from the dropdown menu.
  • Select Role: Choose your user type (Principal, Teacher, Student, or Accountant). The system dynamically adjusts the next screen based on your selected role.
  • Click Continue to proceed to the specific login/registration screen.

2. Principal Access

The Principal acts as the master administrator for the institution.

  • Registration: If setting up the college for the first time, click "Create One" and enter your Name, Email, and Password.
  • Security Lock: Note: Only one Principal account can be registered per college. Once claimed, the system locks out any further Principal registrations for that institution to ensure absolute security.
  • Sign In: Existing Principals simply enter their registered Email and Password.

3. Teacher Access (Room Code Required)

Teachers require a specific, secure code to join their department.

  • Room Code: Both Registration and Sign-In require a 6-digit Room Code. This code is generated by the Principal and provided to the Head of Department (HOD) to share with their respective faculty.
  • The Room Code acts as an automatic routing tool, mapping the teacher directly to their correct department in the database.

4. Student Access (Roll Number Required)

Students must link their accounts to the official college database using their Roll Number.

  • Registration: Students must enter their exact Roll Number as provided by the college. The system verifies this Roll Number against the master database uploaded by the Principal.
  • Name Verification: The name entered during registration must exactly match the name linked to the Roll Number in the database to prevent impersonation.
  • Sign In: Once registered and verified, students simply sign in using their Email and Password.
Important: Email Verification Requirement
When any user (Principal, Teacher, or Student) creates a new account, Adhyora sends a secure verification link to their email address. You cannot sign in until you open that email and click the verification link. If you attempt to log in without verifying, the system will deny access and offer to resend the link.
Forgot Password: If any user forgets their password, they can click "Forgot Password?" to securely dispatch a reset link to their registered email address.

Principal Portal Guide

Welcome to the Adhyora Principal Dashboard. As the master administrator, this interface provides you with complete control over the institution's academic structure, user access, and global settings.

First-Time Login & College Setup Flow

Upon your first successful login as the Principal, you must complete a critical initialization sequence to bring the college online.

1. Security PIN & Biometrics

As the master administrator, you possess absolute authority. A secure lock screen must be established.

  • Create PIN: Enter a secure 4-digit PIN, then re-enter it to confirm. You will use this PIN to authenticate destructive actions (like deleting users) and to unlock the app on future visits.
  • Enable Biometrics: Link your device's fingerprint or Face ID for faster logins. You can click "Skip for now" and configure this later in Settings.

2. The Initialization Workflow

Once security is established, you must follow this exact sequence to configure the college:

  • Step 1 - Data Ingestion: Navigate immediately to the Data Section. Set up the daily bell timings, then upload the Master CSV files for Students, Subjects, and the Academic Calendar. If utilizing the Fee Portal, configure your Razorpay keys and fee structures here.
  • Step 2 - Timetable Framework: Go to the Master Timetable Management panel. Define the structural categories for every period of the week.
  • Step 3 - Faculty Onboarding: Open the Roomcode Manager. Distribute the generated department codes to your respective Heads of Department (HODs) so they can invite their teachers.
  • Step 4 - Teacher Verification: As teachers sign up, go to the Teacher List to approve their accounts and grant HOD status to the department heads.
  • Step 5 - Final Class Assignments: Instruct all approved teachers to declare their subjects. Once complete, return to Assign General Subjects to map faculty to the shared courses (VAC, AECC, etc.).

Data Section Overview

The foundation of your college's Adhyora system is managed in the Data Section. This is where you will configure your college's timings and upload the core database of students, subjects, and academic holidays.

  • To access this area, click the Data Section icon on your main dashboard.
  • Note: Because this section controls sensitive global data, many actions here will require your 4-digit Security PIN to execute.

Timetable Configuration

Before assigning classes, you must define the physical bell schedule for your institution.

  • Click Timetable Setup to open the configuration panel.
  • Period Count: Enter the total number of periods in a standard working day.
  • Start Time & Duration: Enter the time the first bell rings and how many minutes each period lasts.
  • Lunch Break: Define after which period the lunch break occurs, and its duration in minutes.
  • Click Save Configuration. The system will automatically generate the empty daily timeline for all students and teachers based on this math.

Master CSV Uploads

Adhyora uses standard spreadsheet files (.CSV) to ingest thousands of records instantly. You will upload three primary files: Students, Subjects, and the Calendar.

1. Uploading Students

This file registers your student body. When a new academic year begins, you simply upload the new first-year students' CSV using this exact same format.

  • Required Headers: Sl No, Roll No, Name, Department, Year, and Course Type.
  • Click Upload Students and select your file.
  • The system will parse the file and ask for your Security PIN to verify the action. Enter your PIN to finalize the upload to the secure server.

2. Uploading Subjects

This file defines the master curriculum. It maps out which subjects belong to which departments and semesters.

  • Required Headers: Code, Name, Type (e.g., AECC, MJD, CORE), OfferingDept, and Semester.
  • Multiple Semesters: If a subject is taught across multiple semesters, do not list them in the same cell. Instead, place each semester number into its own adjacent column to the right (e.g., Column E: 1, Column F: 2). The system will automatically detect all additional semester columns.
  • General Subjects: If a subject is open to the entire college and not bound to a specific department, list the Offering Department as General.
  • Select the file, verify the system's count, and enter your Security PIN to upload.

3. Uploading the Academic Calendar

This file synchronizes the Attendance Predictor and the global Timetable with your institution's specific holidays and semester bounds.

  • Required Headers: Date (YYYY-MM-DD), Day, Event, and Working.
  • Semester Bounds: You must include rows that explicitly state "START OF ODD", "END OF ODD", "START OF EVEN", and "END OF EVEN" in the event column so the system knows when semesters transition.
  • Once uploaded, the system validates the calendar format. Enter your Security PIN to lock the calendar into the database.

Semester & Academic Year Management

Active Semester Type

Adhyora automatically detects whether the institution is currently in an Odd or Even semester by cross-referencing today's date with the bounds uploaded in your Academic Calendar CSV.

  • If the automatic date calculation is incorrect, or if the college transitions to a new semester ahead of schedule, you can manually override the system.
  • Click the Flip to Odd/Even button. The system will instantly invalidate the local caches of all student and teacher devices, forcing their apps to reload and display the newly selected semester data.

Promote Students (End of Year)

At the conclusion of the academic year, the Principal must initiate the global promotion sequence.

  • Click Promote Semester.
  • The system will automatically shift all students up by one academic year (e.g., Year 1 becomes Year 2).
  • Students in their final year (e.g., Year 3 or 4) are safely graduated and moved to the Alumni database.
  • Note: After the promotion sequence is complete, the institution will have zero 1st Year students. You must use the Master CSV Upload tool to ingest the newly enrolled batch of 1st years.

Financial Integrations (Pro/Ultimate Tiers)

Feature Lock Notice: The Payment Gateway and Semester Fee configurations are exclusively available to institutions operating on the Adhyora Pro or Ultimate plans. These buttons will be locked and inactive if the institution is on the Base plan.

Payment Gateway Link

To collect tuition fees directly from students, the college must link its institutional Razorpay account to Adhyora.

  • Click Link Razorpay to open the API Key configuration window.
  • Enter the Key ID (Public) and the highly sensitive Key Secret obtained from your college's Razorpay Dashboard.
  • Click Secure & Save. The keys are encrypted and stored safely on the cloud database.

Semester Fee Settings

Once the gateway is active, you can define the exact fee requirements for different departments and semesters.

  • Click Open Fee Setup to launch the Institutional Fee Management Center.
  • Select the specific Department, Target Year, and Target Semester you are configuring.
  • Enter the Fee Amount (₹).
  • Set a Payment Due Date deadline. The system automatically defaults to 30 days after the start of the semester (based on calendar data), but this can be adjusted.
  • Click Publish Fee Structure. This immediately pushes the fee ledger to the selected students' portals.

Data Exports & Reports

Administrators can extract the entire academic database into clean, formatted Excel-ready CSV files for offline analysis and institutional record-keeping.

  • Click Open Exporter in the Data Section.
  • Select the specific Department and Academic Year you wish to pull records for.
  • Choose one of the three export types below to generate the file.

Available Export Types

1. Internal Marks (NEP Formatted)
Generates a comprehensive matrix of test scores, assignment grades, and attendance marks for every student in the selected department.

2. Attendance Statistics (Overall & Subject-Wise)
Extracts the total periods conducted versus periods attended, calculating both the strict overall percentage and the individual subject percentages.

3. Daily Attendance Logs (P1 to P6)
The most detailed export available. Provides a granular, day-by-day, period-by-period (P1 through P6) log of exactly when a student was marked Present (P) or Absent (A).

Roomcode Manager (Teacher Access)

Adhyora uses a secure, department-based Roomcode system to onboard faculty. The system automatically identifies your college's departments and course durations based on the Students CSV you uploaded.

Understanding & Sharing Roomcodes

Each department is assigned a unique 6-digit Roomcode. Teachers must enter this code when signing up to automatically join their respective department.

  • Distribution: The Principal shares the specific Roomcode with the Head of Department (HOD). The HOD then distributes it to the other teachers in their department.
  • Share Code: Click the Share icon (network node) next to any department to instantly copy or send the code.

Managing Departments

  • Add Missing Departments: If a department wasn't auto-detected, click the green + Add Dept button at the top right to manually declare its name and duration.
  • Edit Duration: If the course length (e.g., 3 Yrs) is incorrect, click the Edit (Pen) icon to modify it.
  • Delete Department: If a department is no longer supported by the college or was created by mistake, click the red Trash icon to permanently delete it.

Security & Regeneration

Security Lockout Warning: Regenerating a Roomcode acts as a hard security reset.
  • Regenerate Single Dept: Click the Refresh icon next to a department to generate a brand new code. Note: This will instantly log out all existing teachers in that department. They must use the new code to log back in.
  • Regen All: Click the red Regen All button at the top to reset every single department code simultaneously, forcing a global teacher logout across the entire college.

Teacher List & Management

The Teacher List acts as the central hub for managing faculty access and reviewing their academic contributions. When teachers use a Roomcode to sign up, their access request appears here for the Principal to review.

1. Faculty Approvals & HOD Assignment

  • Review Requests: You will see the teacher's Name, Email Address, and the Department they are attempting to join.
  • Approve or Decline: Use the dropdown menu to change their status from Pending to Approved (granting app access) or Declined.
  • Assign HOD: If the teacher is the Head of the Department, check the HOD box next to their name to grant them elevated department privileges.

2. Direct Messaging

You can send official, targeted messages directly to individual teachers from this list.

  • Click the Chat Icon on a teacher's card to open the message popup.
  • Enter a Message Title and your Description.
  • Click Send Broadcast. The teacher will receive this message directly in their personal app Inbox.

Teacher Performance Dashboard

Clicking directly on any approved Teacher's Name Card will open their detailed, individual performance dashboard.

Workload & Timetable Analysis

  • Total Hours Taught: A quick glance at the top right shows the aggregate number of hours this teacher has conducted classes over the selected timeframe.
  • Data Filters: Use the Date Picker to see exactly how many hours were taught on a specific day, or click All Time to view the entire logged history.
  • Subject Breakdown: The left panel itemizes exactly how many hours the teacher has spent teaching each of their assigned subjects.
  • Timetable Assigned: The right panel displays the teacher's weekly schedule. It shows exactly which period they are teaching and displays the Semester Number for that specific class block.

Attendance Timeline Logs

Scroll down the dashboard to view the complete audit trail of this teacher's class records.

  • This chronological timeline records every single period the teacher has submitted attendance for.
  • It displays the Subject Name, the exact Time and Date the attendance was submitted, the Period Number, and the present-to-total ratio for that specific class.

Student List & Profiles

The Student List provides a complete directory of all students enrolled in your institution. From here, you can manage their app access, update their details, and dive deep into their individual academic performance.

1. Managing Student Access & Details

Click the Shield Icon next to any student to open the Admin Edit panel.

  • Correct Names: If a student's name has a typo, you can manually correct it here and save it to the database.
  • Revoke Access: Change the status from Approved to Declined to instantly log the student out of the app and block their access.
  • Delete Student: If a student leaves the college (e.g., Transfer Certificate applied), click the red Delete Student button to permanently remove them from the system.
  • Note: All sensitive actions here require your Security PIN to execute.

2. Direct Messaging

  • Click the Chat Icon on a student's card to send a direct, private message to that specific student. This will appear securely in their app's Inbox.

Student Performance Dashboard

Clicking directly on a Student's Name Card opens their comprehensive academic profile.

Attendance Overview

  • Projected Percentage: The large circular wave visualizes their projected attendance if they attend all remaining classes.
  • Raw Statistics: The cards on the right display the mathematical breakdown of classes held, periods present, and periods absent.

Academics & Curriculum

  • Subject-Wise Attendance: Scroll down to see exactly how the student is performing in each specific subject. You can view All Time data or use the date picker to check if they attended all their assigned classes on a specific date.
  • Internal Marks: Use the dropdown menu to toggle between different examinations (e.g., 1st Internal, 2nd Internal) to view their uploaded scores.
  • Enrolled Subjects: A compiled list of every subject the student is officially enrolled in for the current semester.

Financial Ledger (Pro/Ultimate Tiers)

If your institution utilizes Adhyora's Fee Management system for semester collections, the bottom of the student's profile will display their complete financial ledger.

  • Ledger Summary: View the student's total target dues, total amount paid, and outstanding balance across all semesters based on the structures you published.
  • Online Transactions: If a student pays through the app, the transaction ID, method (Online), and official receipt are automatically logged here.
  • Offline Collection: If a student pays their fees directly at the office in cash or cheque, click the Collect Offline button. Enter your Security PIN to securely log the payment into the cloud database and generate an official digital receipt.

Batch Management & Transfers

The Batch Management panel allows you to view and reorganize students who have been split into multiple batches for a specific subject (e.g., practical labs or divided core classes).

1. Viewing Subject Batches

  • Filter Data: Use the top dropdown menus to select the specific Semester, subject Category, and the exact Subject Name.
  • If the subject has been divided into multiple batches in the Timetable, the system will instantly load the respective batch cards.

2. Inspecting Batch Rosters

  • Expand Cards: Click on any Batch Card (e.g., Batch 1, Batch 2) to expand it and reveal the full student roster.
  • Batch Details: The right side of the card header displays the assigned Teacher's Name and the Room Number.
  • Student Information: Each row displays the student's Name, Roll Number, and Department.

3. Moving Students Between Batches

If a student needs to be transferred to a different batch to balance class sizes or resolve scheduling conflicts, you can execute a secure database transfer.

  • Select Students: Click the checkbox (or anywhere on the student's card) to select one or multiple students from a batch.
  • Initiate Transfer: Once students are selected, the green Move Selected button at the top right will activate. Click it.
  • Select Destination: A popup modal will appear listing all available alternative batches for this subject. The student's current batch will be disabled (greyed out).
  • Execute: Click the desired destination batch to securely transfer the students. The system will automatically update the students' and teachers' devices to reflect the new roster.

Master Timetable Management

The Timetable panel allows the Principal to define the foundational academic structure for the entire college, dictating which types of classes occur during each period.

1. Defining the Structure & Categories

Drawing from the NEP guidelines and the Master Subjects CSV you uploaded, you must set the daily framework for every semester.

  • Select Semester & Day: Use the top dropdown to select the target Semester, and use the Mon - Fri buttons to switch between days.
  • Assign Categories: For each available period, use the dropdown to select the class category (e.g., AECC, MJD 4, TUTORIAL).
  • Save Structure: Once the day is configured, click the green Save Structure button.
Note on Department Autonomy: This global structure acts as a guide. Department HODs can overwrite period categories for their own specific major/core subjects. However, shared general classes (like VAC, SEC, and AECC) strictly follow this principal-defined structure.

2. Assigning General Subjects

General subjects (e.g., "Health Wellness and Yoga") are not managed by a specific department. Therefore, the Principal must manually assign teachers to these classes.

  • After saving a structure, the Assign Classes button will appear at the bottom. Click it to enter the Assignment Panel.
  • Select the Semester and Day. The system will automatically filter and display only the periods that contain General/Shared categories.
  • Select the exact Subject, pick the Teacher from the dropdown, and enter the physical Room location.
Teacher Visibility Rules: A teacher will only appear in the dropdown list for a specific subject if they have successfully signed into the app and added that exact subject to their teaching profile.

3. Splitting Classes into Batches (Multi-Teacher)

If a shared subject has too many students or requires multiple teachers operating in the same period, you must split the class into distinct batches.

  • Click the green Split button on any assigned period.
  • A modal will appear analyzing the students enrolled in this subject across different departments.
  • Select the Total Batches required (e.g., 2 Batches).
  • Map each participating Department to a specific batch (e.g., BSc Computer Science goes to Batch 1).
  • Click Confirm Split. The period card will duplicate into multiple batch cards, allowing you to assign a different Teacher and Room to each batch.

Once all teachers and batches are configured, click the green Save Timetable button at the top right of the screen to lock in the assignments and push the schedule live to the students and faculty.

The Subject Master panel serves as the complete repository of all courses offered at your institution. This list is initially populated by your Master CSV upload but can be manually managed here.

1. Searching & Filtering

  • Use the top search bar to instantly find any subject by typing its Subject Name, Subject Code, or Category/Type (e.g., AECC, CORE, VAC).

2. Editing a Subject

  • If there is a typo or structural change required for a course, click the Edit (Pen) icon on its respective card.
  • Modify the Subject Code, Name, Category, Offering Department, or applicable Semesters.
  • Click Save Changes. You will be prompted to enter your Security PIN to authenticate this modification.

3. Deleting a Subject

  • If a course is permanently removed from the college curriculum, click the red Trash icon.
  • A warning popup will ask you to confirm the deletion of the specific subject and code.
  • Click Yes and provide your Security PIN to execute the secure deletion from the database.

Student Subject Reassignment

The Student Subjects panel provides a granular view of exactly which students are enrolled in specific subjects. It is primarily used to reassign students between elective or language courses (like AECC, SEC, VAC) if they wish to change their curriculum track.

1. Viewing Enrolled Students

  • Navigate to the Students Subjects tab on the main sidebar.
  • Use the dropdown menus to progressively filter by Semester, subject Category (e.g., AECC), and the specific Subject Name.
  • The system will dynamically load a list of all students officially enrolled in that course across all departments.

2. Reassigning Subjects

If a student requests a transfer to a different subject within the same category (e.g., switching from Functional English to Arabic), you can process that move here.

  • Select Students: Check the box next to one or multiple students you wish to transfer.
  • Initiate Move: Click the green Move Selected button at the top right.
  • Select Destination: A popup will appear. Use the dropdown to select the new subject. Note: The system intelligently restricts the destination options to subjects within the exact same category to prevent invalid curriculum assignments.
  • Confirm: Click Confirm Reassignment. You will be prompted to enter your Security PIN to authenticate and finalize the database transfer.

Event Requests & Approvals

The Events panel allows the Principal to review and approve special extracurricular activities or duties (e.g., NSS, NCC, Sports) requested by teachers. Approving an event automatically grants attendance to all participating students for that specific period.

1. Reviewing Event Requests

  • When a teacher submits an event attendance request from their portal, it appears here with a Pending status.
  • The event card displays the Event Name, the Requesting Teacher, the Date, and the specific Period.

2. Inspecting Participants & Approving

  • View Students: Click on any Pending event card to expand it. This reveals the complete roster of students participating in the event, including their Roll Numbers and Departments.
  • Grant Attendance: If the request is valid, click the green Approve Event & Save Attendance button.
  • The system will securely process the transaction, instantly marking all listed students as "Present" for that specific period, overriding any standard class absence.

Once approved, the card's status updates to Accepted. You can still expand the card at any time to review the list of students who were granted attendance.

Live Attendance Tracker

The Attendance Records panel acts as a "God-Mode" tracker for the Principal. It provides a real-time, bird's-eye view of every teacher, their assigned periods, and whether they have submitted their attendance logs for the day.

1. Viewing & Filtering the Grid

  • Day Navigation: Use the top day buttons (Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.) to view the timetable allocations and attendance statuses for different days of the current week.
  • Smart Search: Use the search bar to instantly filter the massive grid. You can type a Teacher's Name, a Department, or even a specific Subject Code/Name to isolate specific records.
  • Subject Tooltips: Hover your mouse over any abbreviated subject code in the grid to view the full name of the subject.

2. The Color-Coded Status System

The system intelligently monitors the current physical time against the college's bell schedule (which you defined in the Data Section) and color-codes each period block accordingly:

  • Black (Default): The class is either currently ongoing or has not yet happened. The deadline to submit attendance has not passed.
  • Green (Verified): The officially assigned teacher successfully marked and submitted the attendance for this period.
  • Yellow (Substitute): Attendance was marked, but by a substitute teacher (someone other than the faculty member assigned in the timetable).
  • Red (Defaulter): The physical time for the period has ended, but the assigned teacher failed to submit the attendance data to the server.

Teacher Portal Guide

Welcome to the Adhyora Teacher Dashboard. This portal equips faculty with powerful tools to manage daily attendance, monitor student records, and handle substitute duties efficiently.

First-Time Login & Security Setup

When you log into the Teacher Portal for the first time, you must complete a brief security and configuration workflow.

1. Account Approval

  • Upon entering your roomcode and signing in, your account is placed in a Pending state.
  • You cannot access the dashboard until the Principal officially approves your account. Once approved, this screen will automatically disappear.

2. Security PIN & Biometrics

To protect sensitive academic data, you are required to set up a secure lock screen.

  • Create PIN: Enter a secure 4-digit PIN, then re-enter it to confirm. You will use this PIN as an extra layer of security to unlock the app on future visits.
  • Enable Biometrics: After setting the PIN, you can optionally link your device's fingerprint or Face ID for faster logins. You can click "Skip for now" and enable it later from your Settings.

3. The Initial Workflow

Once your security is set up, follow these steps to configure your teaching profile:

  • Step 1 (All Teachers): The app will prompt you to declare the subjects you are teaching. Go to the My Subjects Declaration panel to add them.
  • Step 2 (HODs Only): If you are the Head of Department, navigate to the Student Subject Allocation panel to assign students to their respective elective and core subjects.
  • Step 3 (HODs Only): Finally, map your department's teachers to the Principal's master structure using the Timetable & Allocations panel.

Strict Security & Booking Rules:
