Student Guide

Everything you need to know about using your Adhyora student portal.

Getting Started

Before accessing your portal, you must register and sign in through the Adhyora central gateway.

1. The Main Gateway

Upon launching Adhyora, you are greeted by the college selection screen.

  • Select Your College: Choose your institution from the dropdown menu.
  • Select Role: Choose Student as your user type.
  • Click Continue to proceed to the registration or login screen.

2. Student Registration & Sign-In

Students must link their accounts to the official college database using their Roll Number.

  • Registration: Enter your exact Roll Number as provided by your college. The system verifies this Roll Number against the master database.
  • Name Verification: The name you enter must exactly match the name the college registered for your roll number. A single spelling difference will block registration.
  • Sign In: Once registered, sign in using your Email and Password.
Important: Email Verification
After creating your account, Adhyora sends a verification link to your email address. You cannot sign in until you click this link. If you didn't receive it, check your spam/junk folder — and if it's still not there, simply try signing in again with your account; since the link hasn't been verified yet, the system will automatically send you a new one.
Forgot Password? Click "Forgot Password?" to receive a secure reset link at your registered email address.

Student Portal Guide

Welcome to the Adhyora Student Dashboard. This interface is designed to give you real-time visibility into your academic progress, attendance records, and internal marks.

1. Dashboard Overview

Upon logging in, you will land on the main dashboard. At the very top, you will see your Name and Roll Number.

  • Switch Semesters: Use the left and right arrows next to the semester title (e.g., "Semester 1") to view your records from previous or upcoming terms.
  • Overall Statistics: The right side displays your raw numbers — Total Classes Held, Attended, and Absent days.

2. The Attendance Predictor

The large, animated percentage ring in the center of your screen is your Projected Percentage — the attendance you will achieve if you attend every remaining class.

  • Projected Percentage: This number represents your attendance if you attend every remaining class until the end of the semester.
  • Current Percentage: Your actual, real-time percentage as of today is displayed just below the ring.

Using the Leave Predictor Tool

Want to know how a few days off will affect your attendance? Use the Predictor:

  • Open the Tool: Click directly on the animated water ring.
  • Enter Leave Days: Type the number of days you plan to be absent.
  • Calculate: Click "Calculate". The system checks the official college calendar and shows your new predicted percentage.
Color Indicators: Green (Excellent), Yellow (Good), Orange (Average), Red (Critical).

3. Subject Ledger & Date Filtering

Scroll down to see a breakdown of your attendance for every individual subject.

  • Filter by Date: Use the calendar icon (Date Picker) above the subjects to select a specific day.
  • Reset View: Click "All Time" to return to your full semester view.
  • View Timeline (Ledger): Click on any individual subject card to open its Attendance Ledger — showing the exact date, time, period number, and the teacher who marked you.

4. Internal Marks & Curriculum

At the bottom of your dashboard, you can track your academic performance and enrolled syllabus.

  • Check Exam Scores: Use the dropdown to toggle between examinations (e.g., Internal 1, Internal 2). Progress bars visually show your score against the maximum.
  • View Enrolled Subjects: Your official curriculum is displayed here. Core/Major subjects are highlighted with a green badge; Elective/Secondary subjects with a yellow badge.

5. The Navigation Menu

Navigate to all other features of Adhyora using the main menu. The currently selected button is always highlighted.

On Mobile: Swipe horizontally on the bottom bar to reveal hidden navigation buttons.

Assignments & Coursework

The Assignments tab centralizes all homework, projects, and study materials posted by your faculty.

  • Assignment Details: Each card shows the Subject Name, Title, Description, and Teacher's Name.
  • Push Reminders: Adhyora automatically sends you a notification 3 days before the deadline.

Completion & Deadline Status

  • Active: Orange due date — assignment is open.
  • Past Deadline (Closed): Card turns red with a strikethrough and shows "Closed".

The "Mark Done" Button

Note: Mark Done is a personal organization tool only. It does not submit files to the teacher.
  • Click to mark an assignment as complete — it fades and shows a checkmark. Click Undo to revert.

Communications

System Notifications

The Notifications tab receives global updates and alerts from the Adhyora system or your college administration.

Inbox Messages

Your Inbox is for direct messages sent by your faculty or the Principal.

  • Green Border: Message from the Principal.
  • Red Border: Message from a Teacher.

Academics & Records

Daily Schedule (Timetable)

  • Day Navigation: Use the Mon-Fri buttons to toggle between days.
  • Period Details: Each period shows Subject Category, Subject Name, Semester, and Room Number.
  • Free Periods: Periods with no class are marked in muted grey.

Live Period Tracking

The blue vertical progress bar on the left fills downward as the day progresses, showing the current period against your schedule.

Daily Attendance

  • Green P: Present. Red A: Absent. Grey: Not yet marked.
  • Use date controls to view attendance for any past day.

College Calendar

  • Use the left and right arrows to navigate between months.
  • Click any highlighted date to see the reason for the holiday or event.

Fee Portal

If your college has not enabled this feature, this screen will show that the portal is offline. Contact your administration office directly for fee payments.

Institutional Summary

  • Total Due: The overall amount requested by the institution.
  • Total Paid: The amount successfully processed.
  • Remaining: Your outstanding balance.

Semester Payments

  • Each semester block shows Pending, Partial, or Paid status.
  • Click Pay to securely open the Razorpay checkout gateway.
Processing Fees: UPI payments are free. Credit/Debit card and Netbanking payments incur standard Razorpay gateway charges. Pixelaks Technologies and your college are not responsible for these third-party charges.

Settings & Preferences

Account & App Toggles

  • Profile Information: Your Name, Department, and Roll Number. Click "Profile Details" for more.
  • Notifications: Enable or disable push notifications.
  • UI Sounds: Toggle cinematic sound effects on or off.
  • Support & Legal: Links to contact support, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions.

Theme Customization

  • Dark/Light Mode: Switch between bright daytime and sleek Dark Mode.
  • Accent Colors: Choose from Blue, Yellow, Pink, Purple, or Orange to personalize the app.

Device Management

  • View all devices currently signed into your account with login timestamps.
  • Click Kick to immediately sign out any unrecognized device.
